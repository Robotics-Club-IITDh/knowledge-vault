# IIT Dharwad Robotics Club: Competition Timeline (Oct 2026 - Mar 2027)

Last updated: 4 Oct 2026

## How to read this table

- **Order:** Events are listed by start date. Where 2026-27 dates are not announced, the date is estimated from last year's edition and marked "(est.)". Always confirm on the organiser's site.
- **Travel:** "Online" means no travel. "Nearby" means Bengaluru. "Long-haul" means a flight or long train ride. For big national events, long-haul is normal and shouldn't rule an event out.
- **Status:** "Confirmed" means dates and details came from a club mailer or a reliable listing. "Dates est." means dates are based on last year. "Unverified" means details could not be confirmed, so check the organiser's page first.
- **Exam clash** is checked against the institute's semester pattern below. For estimated dates, treat the clash as provisional until dates are announced.
- Anything that looks interesting is worth searching for the latest details.
